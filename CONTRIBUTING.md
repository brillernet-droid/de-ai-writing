# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they improve writing quality, safety, or clarity.

## Good Contributions

- New AI-like wording patterns with concrete examples and safer replacement moves.
- New structure patterns that explain why the structure fails and how to rebuild it.
- Better examples for academic, professional, translated, or personal writing.
- Safety improvements that prevent invented citations, unsupported claims, or over-editing.

## What To Avoid

- Detector-evasion claims or guaranteed "human score" promises.
- Large copied word lists without context.
- Prompts, keys, private product logic, payment logic, or user data examples.
- Real user text unless it is clearly public, anonymized, and permitted.
- Edits that make academic or technical writing casual when the formal register is required.

## Pull Request Checklist

- The change preserves facts, citations, terminology, and material uncertainty.
- The change treats flags as editing signals, not proof of AI generation.
- Examples are synthetic or permission-safe.
- `python3 scripts/validate_repository.py` passes.
