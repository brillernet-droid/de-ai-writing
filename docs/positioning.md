# Project Positioning

## One-Line Position

ToneKeep De-AI Writing Skill helps AI assistants revise prose that sounds generic, defensive, or machine-like while keeping facts, sources, terminology, and the author's intent intact.

## Public Claim

Use:

- "AI-style writing diagnosis"
- "natural rewrite"
- "source-safe editing"
- "voice-preserving writing revision"
- "bilingual de-AI writing workflow"

Avoid:

- "formal AI detector"
- "plagiarism checker"
- "guaranteed AI-rate reduction"
- "bypass detector"
- "undetectable AI writing"

## Differentiation

Most writing tools focus on fluency. This skill focuses on authorship signals:

- Does the paragraph start with real substance or generic context?
- Are claims attached to actors, mechanisms, evidence, or consequences?
- Is uncertainty stated once and correctly, rather than repeated defensively?
- Does the structure fit the genre, or does it follow a generic AI template?
- Does the rewrite preserve the author's register rather than making everything casual?

## Relationship To ToneKeep

ToneKeep is the product brand. This repository is the public skill edition.

The public edition explains the editing method and provides a lightweight workflow. It should not include the private ToneKeep product backend, user database, scoring weights, payment logic, production prompts, or training corpus.

## Target Users

- Students and researchers revising English abstracts, introductions, discussions, or cover letters.
- Applicants writing personal statements and emails.
- Product and business writers who want less generic copy.
- Chinese-English writers who need natural English logic rather than literal translation.
- AI builders who want a reusable prompt-skill pattern for source-safe writing improvement.
