# Example: Source-Safe Audit

## Prompt

```text
Use $de-ai-writing source-safe mode. Do not rewrite unsupported claims as facts.
```

## Draft

```text
Studies show that AI-generated writing is now the main reason students lose marks in academic writing courses, and experts agree that all universities will soon require AI detection reports.
```

## Audit

| Claim or phrase | Why it needs support | Safe edit or verification step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Studies show` | No study is named. | Cite a specific study or narrow the claim. |
| `main reason students lose marks` | Strong ranking claim without evidence. | Replace with a bounded statement if supported by course data. |
| `experts agree` | Vague collective authority. | Name the expert body or remove the authority claim. |
| `all universities will soon require` | Universal forecast without source. | Verify against policy documents or state it as an open question. |
