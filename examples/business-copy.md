# Example: Business Copy Rewrite

## Prompt

```text
Use $de-ai-writing standard mode. Make this product copy more concrete and less generic.
```

## Before

```text
Our cutting-edge platform empowers teams to unlock actionable insights through a seamless and intuitive experience. By leveraging robust analytics, users can optimize workflows and drive transformative outcomes.
```

## Diagnosis

- Phrasing: `cutting-edge`, `empowers`, `unlock actionable insights`, `seamless`, `robust`, and `transformative outcomes` are unsupported praise.
- Structure: the copy claims value before naming the feature or user task.
- Precision: the sentence does not say which workflow changes or decisions improve.

## After

```text
The platform turns support tickets, customer notes, and call summaries into a searchable issue board. Teams can group repeated complaints, assign owners, and track whether fixes reduce the same problem in later conversations.
```

## Note

The revision replaces praise with observable product behavior.
