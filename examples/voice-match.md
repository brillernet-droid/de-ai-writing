# Example: Voice Match

## Prompt

```text
Use $de-ai-writing voice-match mode. Infer the author's habits from the sample, then revise the draft.
```

## Voice Sample

```text
I do not need the paragraph to sound impressive. I need it to say exactly what happened, why it matters, and what still needs checking.
```

## Draft

```text
This innovative workflow represents a transformative step toward enhancing the overall quality of research writing by providing a holistic approach to AI-assisted revision.
```

## After

```text
This workflow does one specific job: it checks whether an AI-assisted draft still says what the writer meant. It flags vague wording, unsupported claims, and structure that sounds too automatic. The next step is to test whether users actually find the revision more useful.
```

## Retained Voice

- Direct sentence openings.
- Plain verbs instead of abstract praise.
- Clear distinction between current behavior and what still needs testing.
