# Example: Chinese-English Translation Naturalisation

## Prompt

```text
Use $de-ai-writing translation-naturalise mode. Keep the meaning, but rewrite the English so it does not mirror Chinese sentence order.
```

## Before

```text
With the continuous development of artificial intelligence technology, English academic writing has important significance for graduate students, and how to improve writing quality has become a problem worth paying attention to.
```

## Diagnosis

- Phrasing: `continuous development`, `important significance`, and `worth paying attention to` are literal and generic.
- Structure: the sentence follows a Chinese policy-style opening rather than English argument logic.
- Precision: the actual problem is not AI development in general, but graduate students revising academic English.

## After

```text
Graduate students increasingly use AI tools when drafting academic English, but many drafts remain generic, over-polished, or weakly tied to evidence. The practical problem is not fluency alone; it is whether the final text still reflects the writer's claim, source material, and disciplinary register.
```

## Note

The revision rebuilds the English argument instead of translating each source phrase directly.
