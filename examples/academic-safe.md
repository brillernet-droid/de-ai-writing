# Example: Academic-Safe Rewrite

## Prompt

```text
Use $de-ai-writing academic-safe mode. Preserve the claim scope and formal register, but remove AI-like phrasing and generic structure.
```

## Before

```text
In today's rapidly evolving research landscape, adolescent physical fitness plays a crucial role in shaping cognitive development. This study provides valuable insights into the complex interplay between cardiopulmonary fitness and executive function, opening new avenues for future research and intervention.
```

## Diagnosis

- Phrasing: `rapidly evolving research landscape`, `plays a crucial role`, `provides valuable insights`, and `opening new avenues` are generic academic filler.
- Structure: the paragraph starts with broad importance before stating a concrete claim.
- Precision: the relationship between fitness and cognition is not specified.

## After

```text
This study examines whether cardiopulmonary fitness is associated with executive function in adolescents. Rather than treating fitness as a general marker of health, the analysis focuses on how variation in aerobic capacity relates to task performance in this sample.
```

## Note

The revision keeps the claim observational. It does not turn association into causation or invent results.
